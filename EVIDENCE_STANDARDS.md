# What counts as evidence?

Anything appropriately authenticated that bears on a **precise claim** may be examined, but admission to review is not automatic belief. Arguments also matter, yet even perfectly valid reasoning cannot turn unjustified premises into established facts.

## Domain-appropriate scrutiny

| Area | Useful checks | Common overreach |
|---|---|---|
| Scientific/empirical | Design, measurement, controls, alternatives, uncertainty, independent repetition when feasible | A physical explanation alone does not prove metaphysical physicalism. |
| Historical/textual | Origin, dating, genre, preservation, translation, independent witnesses, corroboration | A record of an event claim does not automatically establish the event. |
| Philosophical/metaphysical | Definitions, premises, validity, countermodels, alternative grounds | Valid form does not prove its premises. |
| Experience/testimony | What was experienced, credibility, context, corroboration, other causes | A sincere experience does not uniquely prove its interpretation. |
| Logic/mathematics | Rules, axioms, proof checking, applicability | A theorem does not automatically describe the physical world. |
| Ethical/evaluative | Normative premises, cases, consequences, rival frameworks | Evidence of harm alone does not settle ultimate moral grounding. |

## Three separate judgments

1. **Source/evidence quality:** Is it trustworthy for this use? What are its limits and lineage?
2. **Direction:** Does it distinguish the specific claim from specified rivals, disfavor it, or favor neither?
3. **Overall confidence:** After all evidence, objections, dependencies, omissions and alternatives, how much warrant is there?

Ten articles repeating one dataset do not become ten independent experiments. Missing evidence matters only when it should reasonably have been detected and preserved. We do not award universal source-type points or invent probabilities. If a source is unread, restricted or inaccessible, we say so.

Findings will link to specific claim/evidence records as they are independently examined and publicly approved.
