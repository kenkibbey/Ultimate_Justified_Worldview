# How to challenge UJW

You may challenge a finding, a method, an assumption, an omitted worldview or **the entire project**. We welcome beginners, experts and people from different cultural and religious settings; no formal credentials or word count are required.

1. Choose the most relevant [question](QUESTIONS.md), or use the [whole-project challenge](https://github.com/kenkibbey/Ultimate_Justified_Worldview/issues/new?template=01-whole-project.yml).
2. Write one natural response: what claim or problem matters, what reasons/evidence you offer, alternatives, or proposed changes. Guiding prompts are **optional**. GitHub separately requires an issue title.
3. We triage for relevance and safety, clarify precise wording when helpful, and identify source access and affected claims.
4. For substantive review, we reconstruct the strongest retain and revise cases, examine evidence against explicit rivals, and record the reasons for a scoped outcome.
5. A challenge may be sustained, partially sustained, unresolved or answered within a stated scope. New evidence or errors can reopen it. We do not delete defeated arguments from history merely to make the synthesis look neat.

**No guaranteed review deadline.** Research capacity is limited and may change. Disagreement is not itself misconduct. Criticism is judged for reasons, not popularity. AI-generated challenges are not independent expert review.

**Public posting:** GitHub issues and their authors' account identities are public. Please avoid sensitive personal details, private files, credentials and restricted cultural material. We do not claim an anonymous or confidential submission path exists.
