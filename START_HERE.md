<h1 align="center">Start here</h1>

**What is UJW?** A worldwide invitation to test ideas about reality rather than defend a predetermined answer.

## How to submit a contribution

1. [Pick a question](QUESTIONS.md), [challenge the whole project](https://github.com/kenkibbey/Ultimate_Justified_Worldview/issues/new?template=01-whole-project.yml), or [suggest something missing](https://github.com/kenkibbey/Ultimate_Justified_Worldview/issues/new?template=08-other-evidence.yml).
2. Tap its **Contribute** link. GitHub generally requires you to sign in or create an account before posting.
3. Give your issue a short title. For example, *Can memory sometimes justify knowledge of the past?*
4. Write your complete thought in **the one required open response box**. Suggested prompts are optional. You may include sources, but you need not have any.
5. Acknowledge that your issue **and GitHub identity will be public**, then submit.

If an issue link does not work in your app, use the [issue chooser](https://github.com/kenkibbey/Ultimate_Justified_Worldview/issues/new/choose) in a browser. There is no minimum word count, special philosophical vocabulary, or compulsory AI audit.

## What could I write?

These are **hypothetical examples**, not verified evidence, actual submissions, or UJW findings.

- **Question:** "How could we check whether an experience shows something outside our mind or only a convincing appearance?"
- **Proposed claim:** "I think some eyewitness accounts justify beliefs about history. What makes their evidence reliable, and what challenges it?"
- **Method challenge:** "If UJW uses logic to test logic, is it relying on circular reasoning?"
- **Missing question:** "Could beauty tell us something important about reality? What would justify that?"

One clear paragraph is enough to begin. You can admit uncertainty or propose a test instead of announcing a conclusion.

## What happens next?

Your submission is a proposal, **not an admitted finding**. It may lead to clarification, source review, comparison with strong rival explanations, and a scoped outcome. Only sufficiently examined and approved conclusions enter the [Current UJW Synthesis](LIVING_WORLDVIEW.md). Full review is not guaranteed on a deadline.

[See the complete explanation](HOW_FINDINGS_WORK.md) · [Glossary](GLOSSARY.md)

Start with one of the [six public questions](QUESTIONS.md). Tell us your claim, objection, experience, argument, source, or alternative explanation. You do not need special credentials. You can also [challenge the entire project](https://github.com/kenkibbey/Ultimate_Justified_Worldview/issues/new?template=01-whole-project.yml).

Our submission form has just **one required writing box**. Guiding prompts are suggestions, not a questionnaire. GitHub will also ask for the issue title and acknowledgment that the submission is public.

**Want to test your challenge first?** Open the [Official AI Auditor Prompt](OFFICIAL_AI_AUDITOR_PROMPT.md), copy it into an AI, and add your challenge. The revision-resistance audit considers the strongest arguments for keeping and revising the current position. This step is optional.

**Before posting:** Please remove personal identifiers, private correspondence, restricted cultural knowledge and other sensitive information. Your GitHub account and issue will be visible to the public.

We may first clarify the claim and ask for sources before deciding whether it merits a full research examination. We cannot promise a review deadline. Ordinary-language contributions are welcome, and disagreement with the current picture is allowed.

The [current picture](LIVING_WORLDVIEW.md) has no admitted substantive worldview findings yet. [Research method](METHOD_AND_FAIRNESS.md) · [Evidence guide](EVIDENCE_STANDARDS.md) · [How challenges work](CHALLENGE_PROCESS.md).
