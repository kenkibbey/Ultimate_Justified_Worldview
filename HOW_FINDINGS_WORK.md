<h1 align="center">How an idea can become a UJW finding</h1>

**You offer a puzzle piece → We clarify it → We examine both sides → We record a limited outcome → An adequately supported claim may enter the living synthesis after approval.**

This is a short explanation of the [full provisional method](METHOD_AND_FAIRNESS.md), which itself can be challenged.

## 1. Submit an idea

A person contributes a claim, objection, source, experience, alternative worldview, or challenge to the entire project through a [public GitHub form](QUESTIONS.md). The issue is a **proposal**, not an established truth. No degree or AI test is required.

## 2. Clarify the precise claim

We ask what exactly is being said, what evidence would matter, and which serious alternative explanations compete with it. A broad label (such as "theism" or "naturalism") is not a single precise claim.

## 3. Examine evidence and objections on both sides

We distinguish three questions:

1. **Quality:** Is this information dependable? Where did it come from? Is it independent of other sources?
2. **Direction:** Does it favor this exact claim over specified rivals, challenge it, or fail to distinguish them?
3. **Confidence:** After examining all relevant independent material and objections, how strongly is the conclusion warranted?

Scientific, historical, philosophical, experiential, logical and ethical claims require methods suited to their domains. A reported experience is not automatically proof of its proposed cause. AIs agreeing with one another are not independent human review.

## 4. Record a careful status

A claim can be **proposed**, **examining**, **provisionally supported**, **unresolved**, **disfavored**, **contradicted within a defined scope**, **withdrawn**, or **superseded**. Challenges may be sustained, partly sustained, unresolved, or answered within a stated scope.

**Not proven is not the same as disproven.** Showing that one formulation fails does not automatically refute every related worldview. Strong surviving objections should remain visible.

## 5. Update the living picture only if warranted

Only sufficiently examined, provisionally supported claims may enter the [Current UJW Synthesis](LIVING_WORLDVIEW.md) through authorized publication. Approved findings should show exact wording, sources, rivals, objections, limits, confidence, dependencies and reasons for revision in the [claim index](CLAIM_INDEX.md) and [public changelog](CHANGELOG.md).

If a supporting claim fails, dependent conclusions must be reconsidered. A popular issue, coherent story, or AI verdict does not automatically become a UJW finding.

## What can someone contribute now?

The project has **no admitted substantive worldview finding yet**. That is a record of the present research stage, not proof that nothing can be known.

Contributors can identify good sources, propose a precise question, challenge our starting assumptions, suggest serious alternatives, or correct an error. Every well-supported correction or discovery may add a useful piece of the puzzle, even if the overall picture remains incomplete.
