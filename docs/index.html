Two-Sided Revision-Resistance Auditor · v0.1
One-tap copying for the complete, unabridged UJW prompt.

How to use: Tap Copy entire prompt, open your preferred AI, paste it, and add your challenge or evidence. The auditor tests both keeping and revising a conclusion. Its advice is not an official UJW decision, and you can submit an issue without running it.

Before you copy: start with a clean AI session

Use a fresh, unprimed AI conversation for the fairest test. Ideally open a Temporary Chat, guest session or comparable mode that does not use earlier conversations or memory, if your AI provider offers one.

  1. Use a new conversation and, where possible, disable memory, personalization, custom instructions and other connected personal context.
  2. Paste this entire auditor prompt first. Do not pre-instruct the AI about your personal beliefs or desired conclusion. Afterwards, give it your challenge and the relevant UJW sources as evidence to examine, not competing instructions.
  3. Ask the AI to expose assumptions and assess the best arguments for retaining and revising the UJW position. Disclose any session settings you could not disable.

Important: An Incognito/Private browser window by itself does not guarantee a signed-in AI stops using account personalization or memory. A fresh session reduces historical priming but no AI is completely unbiased, neutral, or free of system and training influences. This audit is optional; you can submit a challenge without it.
