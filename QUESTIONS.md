<h1 align="center">Six open research questions</h1>

**Current stage:** No substantive UJW finding has yet been admitted for any of these questions. This records completed project work, not a verdict about reality. [First-time guide](START_HERE.md) · [How our review works](HOW_FINDINGS_WORK.md).

## 1. What can we know, and how can we know it?

How can we tell when the senses, reasoning, memory, experience or testimony deserve trust? What could separate knowledge from illusion or good guessing?

*Possible contribution:* Suggest a way to test a belief, or challenge that test.

[Submit a claim, argument, challenge, or evidence](https://github.com/kenkibbey/Ultimate_Justified_Worldview/issues/new?template=02-knowledge-method.yml)

## 2. What is reality, and why, if anything, does it exist?

Consider serious theistic, nontheistic, naturalistic, idealist and other explanations without assuming one is right.

*Possible contribution:* State one precise explanation and ask what could distinguish it from the strongest rival.

[Submit a claim, argument, challenge, or evidence](https://github.com/kenkibbey/Ultimate_Justified_Worldview/issues/new?template=03-reality-existence.yml)

## 3. What are we, and how did we come to exist?

Explore human origins, bodies, consciousness, minds and identity; distinguish scientific observations from broader interpretations.

*Possible contribution:* Ask what one observation can really show about consciousness.

[Submit a claim, argument, challenge, or evidence](https://github.com/kenkibbey/Ultimate_Justified_Worldview/issues/new?template=04-human-consciousness.yml)

## 4. What, if anything, gives life meaning or purpose?

Is meaning part of reality, something people make, or understood differently in different traditions?

*Possible contribution:* Compare two meanings of 'purpose' and how each might be justified.

[Submit a claim, argument, challenge, or evidence](https://github.com/kenkibbey/Ultimate_Justified_Worldview/issues/new?template=05-meaning-purpose.yml)

## 5. How should we live, and why?

Explore right and wrong, justice, duty and well-being, and the difference between what people do and what they ought to do.

*Possible contribution:* Offer a moral rule and a difficult counterexample.

[Submit a claim, argument, challenge, or evidence](https://github.com/kenkibbey/Ultimate_Justified_Worldview/issues/new?template=06-morality-action.yml)

## 6. What, if anything, happens after death?

What do scientific findings, philosophy, historical sources, traditions and reports actually support about death and possible survival?

*Possible contribution:* Separate a reported experience from proposed causes.

[Submit a claim, argument, challenge, or evidence](https://github.com/kenkibbey/Ultimate_Justified_Worldview/issues/new?template=07-death-destiny.yml)

## Challenge the project itself

[Challenge the foundations, methods, rules or entire synthesis](https://github.com/kenkibbey/Ultimate_Justified_Worldview/issues/new?template=01-whole-project.yml).

## Another important question?

[Offer evidence or suggest a missing question](https://github.com/kenkibbey/Ultimate_Justified_Worldview/issues/new?template=08-other-evidence.yml).

We will also study history, cosmos, consciousness, suffering, beauty, miracles, claimed revelation, religions and other traditions in context, and whether the questions themselves should change.
