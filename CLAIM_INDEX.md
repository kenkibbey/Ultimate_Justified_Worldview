<h1 align="center">Claim and evidence index</h1>

**Status:** Founding stage. No approved public claim dossier or synthesis result yet.

The private research workspace uses permanent IDs for questions (UJW-Q), candidate formulations (UJW-W), claims (UJW-CLM), sources (UJW-SRC), evidential units (UJW-EVD), arguments (UJW-ARG), examinations (UJW-TEST), challenges (UJW-CH), synthesis entries (UJW-SYN), revisions (UJW-REV), and releases (UJW-PUB).

For each future public result, expect: the exact scoped proposition; relevant versions; whether it is essential/disputed/optional; strongest reasons for and against; source links or accurate source access descriptions; evidence quality; direction relative to explicit rivals; confidence and dependencies; status; reviewer and revision history; what could change the conclusion.

**No private source files or identities are implied to be publicly accessible.** We will publish verified citations and permitted summaries when a claim is approved for release.
