<h1 align="center">Plain-language UJW glossary</h1>

These are **working explanations**, not final philosophical verdicts. Their adequacy can be challenged.

**Claim:** A precise statement that might be true or false.

**Truth:** Whether the claim really matches reality, regardless of what people believe.

**Evidence:** Information relevant to evaluating a claim. Evidence that an experience was reported does not automatically prove a proposed cause.

**Justification / warrant:** Reasons or evidence strong enough to support a belief after considering objections. A justified belief can still be mistaken.

**Knowledge:** What it means to genuinely know something, rather than only to believe it; the definition is itself an open research issue.

**Confidence:** How strongly the overall available reasons currently support an exact claim, taking limitations into account.

**Source independence:** Whether separate reports or studies actually contain distinct evidence, rather than copies of one source.

**Rival explanation:** Another serious way of explaining the same observations.

**Coherence:** An explanation's parts fit together without contradiction. Coherence alone cannot show whether it is true.

**Scope:** The conditions or limits to which a conclusion applies.

**Assumption:** A starting idea used provisionally that may itself need to be tested.

**Dependency:** When one conclusion relies on another claim; if the foundation changes, dependent conclusions may need revision.

**Steelman:** Give an opposing position its strongest reasonable interpretation, not an easy-to-dismiss caricature.

**Provisional finding:** A carefully qualified finding presently warranted by adequate review but open to correction.

**Unresolved:** Current reasons are insufficient to settle the claim against its serious alternatives; this is not proof that all alternatives are equally likely.

**Disfavored versus disproven:** A claim may currently have weaker support without being conclusively refuted. Contradiction must be stated within the actual tested scope.

**Living synthesis:** UJW's connected, revisable set of approved supported findings and clearly visible uncertainties; not every idea posted to GitHub.

**Issue:** A public GitHub record where someone submits an argument, question, source or challenge. Posting is not proof.

**AI auditor:** An optional set of AI instructions for testing the strongest arguments for both keeping and revising a position. AI output is advisory, not independent human peer review.
