## Our current best-justified picture of reality

**No substantive UJW claim has yet completed the independent-source examination required to enter this living synthesis.** Therefore we do not currently affirm an overall worldview, religion, philosophical system, or account of ultimate reality as UJW's established conclusion. This does **not** mean nothing can be known; it means our project's testing and public justification record is just beginning. An unsettled question is not automatically evidence against an answer.

## What happens to incoming challenges?

1. A person submits a [public challenge or proposed evidence](https://github.com/kenkibbey/Ultimate_Justified_Worldview/issues/new/choose). That submission is a proposal, not a finding.
2. Reviewers clarify the precise claim and the strongest reasoning in favor of and against it. They trace source independence and consider relevant alternatives.
3. A documented, scoped decision marks the claim supported, unresolved, disfavored, defeated within specified assumptions, or reopened. We distinguish not-proven from disproved.
4. Only an **adequately examined, provisionally supported** claim may enter this synthesis. Its exact wording, evidence, objections, confidence, limits and dependencies will be linked to the [claim index](CLAIM_INDEX.md).
5. If a challenge defeats or changes a supporting premise, dependent conclusions are reopened, earlier versions remain traceable, and a new release records the change in the [changelog](CHANGELOG.md).

**The living synthesis is a carefully reviewed public snapshot, not an automatically generated consensus from issue traffic.** Popularity, coherent storytelling, and agreement among AIs are not independent confirmation. Changes require research and authorized publication. See [methods](METHOD_AND_FAIRNESS.md) and [evidence standards](EVIDENCE_STANDARDS.md).

## The active frontier

Our first bounded inquiry asks whether human beings can obtain justified knowledge of reality, and how genuine knowledge might be distinguished from convincing illusion. Rival views, including radical skepticism and theistic and nontheistic accounts, must be examined on their strongest formulations. The methods for judging them remain open to challenge.
