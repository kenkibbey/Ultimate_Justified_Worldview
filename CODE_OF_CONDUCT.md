# Community conduct

Challenge ideas vigorously; treat people respectfully. Represent opposing claims in their strongest fair form. No harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing, coercive disclosure, impersonation or posting of restricted/private information.

Moderators may ask for clarification, limit off-topic/unsafe threads, or close submissions for safety. Moderation is not proof a claim is false. Where practical, reasoned appeals about substance or process can be submitted through the whole-project challenge form. This document is provisional and reviewable.

Please do not assume public GitHub issues are confidential. Do not provide protected personal, cultural or copyrighted materials without permission.
