<h1 align="center">Contributing</h1>

You can help by offering one clear claim, question, source, counterargument, personal observation, correction, or alternative worldview, and explaining why it might matter. You need not know academic jargon. If possible, include original source links, editions and locations so others can inspect your reasons.

Please submit through the relevant [six-question issue form](QUESTIONS.md), the [whole-project challenge](https://github.com/kenkibbey/Ultimate_Justified_Worldview/issues/new?template=01-whole-project.yml), or the [other evidence form](https://github.com/kenkibbey/Ultimate_Justified_Worldview/issues/new?template=08-other-evidence.yml).

One written response box is required; optional prompts are suggestions. We may ask clarifying questions. We will not assume your position is right or wrong because of identity, tradition, academic rank or rhetorical style. Distinguish first-hand experiences from causal interpretations. Respect source rights and community restrictions.

Read the [challenge process](CHALLENGE_PROCESS.md) and [community conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). Participation is publicly identifiable on GitHub; never post secrets or sensitive details. We do not promise prompt full reviews.
